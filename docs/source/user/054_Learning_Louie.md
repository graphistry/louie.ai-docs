# Learning Louie

Louie improves at your work through two kinds of stored knowledge, both of which
you add deliberately — nothing is learned implicitly from your usage.

| | For | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| **Examples** | Simple facts and short patterns worth recalling | System or organization |
| **Recipes** | Multi-step workflows repeated consistently | System or organization |

Both come in two levels: **system-level**, editable by admins and available to
everyone, and **organization-level**, editable by org admins and available to
that organization.

See [Examples](056_System_level__For_all_users__only_admin_editable.md) and
[Recipes](057_Recipes.md) for how to list, author and save each.

Knowledge bases are also consulted — see
[Louie Uses and Learns AI Knowledge Bases](055_Louie_uses_and_learns_AI_knowledge_bases.md).
